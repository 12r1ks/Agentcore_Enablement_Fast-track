# Lumora Home Electronics: Company Information

> Fictional company created for a training project. All names, addresses, emails and numbers are invented.

## About Lumora

Lumora Home Electronics is an online retailer of smart home devices: speakers, lighting, security cameras, thermostats and accessories. Lumora was founded in 2019 and sells to customers in the European Union and the United Kingdom. It ships from two warehouses, one in Rotterdam (Netherlands) and one in Leipzig (Germany).

## Contact and Support Hours

- Customer support email: support@lumora.example.com
- Phone: +31 20 555 0100 (Netherlands)
- Support hours: Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00 CET. Closed on public holidays in the Netherlands.
- The AI support agent is available 24/7. Requests that it cannot resolve are passed to a human agent, who answers during support hours.
- Typical human response time: within 1 business day.

## Order Statuses

Every order has exactly one status:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| PLACED | Order received and payment confirmed. Not yet picked in the warehouse. |
| PROCESSING | Items are being picked and packed. Usually takes 1 business day. |
| SHIPPED | Handed to the carrier. A tracking number is available. |
| DELAYED | The order is later than the promised delivery date. The delay reason is recorded on the order. |
| DELIVERED | The carrier confirmed delivery. |
| CANCELLED | The order was cancelled before shipping. |
| REFUNDED | The full order amount was returned to the customer. |
| PARTIALLY_REFUNDED | Part of the order amount was returned to the customer. |

## Shipping and Delivery

- Standard shipping: 3 to 5 business days after the order ships. Free for orders of 50 EUR or more, otherwise 4.90 EUR.
- Express shipping: 1 to 2 business days after the order ships. Costs 9.90 EUR.
- Orders placed before 14:00 CET on a business day are usually shipped the same day.
- Carriers: PostNL for the Netherlands and Belgium, DHL for Germany and the rest of the EU, Royal Mail for the United Kingdom.
- Customers receive a tracking link by email when the order ships.

## Common Reasons for Delays

When an order is DELAYED, the reason is one of the following:

- **Carrier backlog:** the carrier has more parcels than usual, often during sales events and the holiday season. Usually adds 1 to 3 business days.
- **Customs hold:** applies to UK orders. Usually adds 2 to 5 business days.
- **Out of stock at warehouse:** an item sold out before it was picked. The order ships when stock arrives, normally within 7 days.
- **Address verification:** the delivery address is incomplete, and the customer must confirm it.
- **Weather or force majeure:** severe weather or strikes interrupt transport.

For a delayed order, support can explain the reason and expected new delivery date. Orders more than 7 days late qualify for a shipping fee refund.

## Returns Policy

- Customers can return most items within 30 days of delivery.
- Items must be unused, in the original packaging, with all accessories.
- Return shipping is free for faulty items. For change-of-mind returns, the customer pays return shipping (up to 6.90 EUR).
- Not returnable: opened software licences, gift cards, and items damaged by the customer.
- A return is registered through support, which sends a prepaid label or return instructions.

## Refund Policy

- Refunds go back to the original payment method.
- The refund is issued within 5 business days after the returned item is received and checked. Bank processing can add another 3 to 5 business days.
- **Refund authorization limits:**
  - Refunds of **1,000 EUR or less** can be approved by the AI support agent.
  - Refunds **over 1,000 EUR** are never approved automatically. They need review by a human support manager.
- Each refund request has a unique operation ID. Repeating a request with the same operation ID does not create a second refund.
- A refund can never be larger than the amount paid for the order, minus any amount already refunded.
- Shipping fees are refunded only if the item was faulty, the order was more than 7 days late, or Lumora made an error.

## Warranty

- All devices include a 2-year warranty that covers manufacturing defects, as required by EU consumer law.
- The warranty does not cover physical damage, water damage (unless the product is rated for it), or unauthorized repairs.
- For a warranty claim, the customer needs the order number and a short description or photo of the problem.
- Lumora repairs, replaces or refunds a faulty product within 30 days of accepting the claim.

## Payment Methods

- Credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard)
- iDEAL, SEPA bank transfer, PayPal
- Lumora does not store full card numbers. Payments are handled by a third-party payment provider.

## Privacy and Data Handling

- Lumora stores the customer's name, email, delivery address, and order history to run the service.
- Support agents, human or AI, may see only the data needed to answer the request.
- Customers can ask for a copy or deletion of their data by emailing privacy@lumora.example.com. Requests are handled within 30 days.
- Support never asks customers for their full card number or password.

## Escalation to a Human Agent

The AI agent hands over to a human in these cases:

- Refund requests over 1,000 EUR
- A customer asks to speak to a person
- Legal threats, safety concerns, or suspected fraud
- Three failed attempts to resolve the same problem in one conversation
- A system error that prevents the agent from checking the order

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I change the delivery address after ordering?**
Yes, while the order is PLACED or PROCESSING. After it has SHIPPED, the address can be changed only through the carrier.

**Can I cancel an order?**
Yes, until it ships. After shipping, use the returns process instead.

**How do I track my order?**
Use the tracking link in the shipping email, or ask support with your order number.

**My device arrived broken. What now?**
Contact support with the order number and a photo. Faulty items are returned free of charge and can be replaced or refunded.

**Do you ship outside the EU and UK?**
Not at the moment.
